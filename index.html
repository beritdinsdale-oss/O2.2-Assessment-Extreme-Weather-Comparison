What changes?

Weather view: the atmosphere contains a certain amount of water vapor available to a storm.

Climate lens: warmer air can hold more water vapor. When a storm forms, that larger moisture supply can contribute to heavier rainfall.

Variable in the visual: available atmospheric moisture

When does weather become a climate pattern?

Move from one year to decades. The same noisy observations look different when you change the time scale.

One yearSeveral yearsDecades

One year: mostly weather. A single year cannot establish a climate trend.

Extreme heat: long-term U.S. records show heat waves becoming more frequent, longer, and more intense.

Climate is the pattern.

Climate change is altering the likelihood and intensity of some extreme weather. The effects vary by event, season, and location—and long-term records are how we see the change.
