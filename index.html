STOP 3 · THE BIG CLIMATE PICTURE

Why Can Extreme Weather Change?

Climate change can alter the conditions that produce extreme weather—but not every extreme changes in the same way or in every place. Explore four examples.

EXTREME HEAT

A warmer starting point changes the odds

Weather still varies from day to day. But when the whole temperature range shifts warmer, temperatures that once sat at the far edge of the curve are reached more often.

→

The key idea: A relatively small shift in average temperature can produce a much larger change in the frequency of extreme heat.

WHY LOCATION & SEASON MATTER

The mechanism is broad; the size of the change is regional.

Local climate, elevation, distance from the ocean, and weather patterns affect how much extreme heat changes from one place to another.

Why aren't extreme-weather changes the same everywhere?

Moisture sources, mountains, storm tracks, season, and the climate a region starts with all shape its extremes. That's why evidence from one U.S. region cannot simply be copied to another.
